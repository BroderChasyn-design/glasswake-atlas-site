# Third-party asset credits

- **whtdragon and hiddenone** — additional RPG Maker MV generator parts (Kitchen Sink and July 2018 ReStaff packs). See `img/generator/CREDITS.md` and its preserved original terms.
- **hiddenone** — beastmen body pieces from Kitchen Sink, in `img/generator-extra/Beastmen Body pieces/`.
- **Avery** — creator credited in the beastmen body filenames (`byAvery`).
- **BroderChasyn** — project integration and curation of the downloaded character generator assets.
- **Kadokawa / Gotcha Gotcha Games** — RPG Maker MV engine and generator format.

## Character creation interface

- **hutaolov3r (hannah hanoi gi)** — “Character Creator | hutaolov3r” Steam Workshop interface, English script `js/hl-cc-ui.js`. The original author header is preserved. [Workshop item](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3816325104).

## RPG Maker MV plugins

- **unordinary (C. Moses), published by ePi** — `WASD_keyboard.js`, from [Control your characters with WASD!](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1530427452). The mapping is adjusted so both WASD and arrow keys move the character.
- **Makai Rosi** — `maka-globalStocks.js`, global shop stock limits. Its original header and free commercial/noncommercial use terms are preserved. [Workshop item](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2837477926).
- **Brian Howard** — `ExitGame.js`, adds an Exit option to the desktop title and game-end menus. [Workshop item](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=906332183).
